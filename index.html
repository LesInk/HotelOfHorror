HOW TO SURVIVE

YOU WAKE ON LEVEL 10. CHOOSE EASY FOR 200 TURNS, MEDIUM FOR 150, OR HARD FOR 100. FIND THE FRONT DOOR ON LEVEL 1 AND ESCAPE.

MOVE ALONG EACH HALLWAY, LISTEN AT DOORS, AND SEARCH ROOMS FOR FOOD, KEYS, AND WEAPONS. CREATURES MAY BE WAITING.

LOCKED STAIRWELLS BLOCK LEVELS 6 AND 3. USE A KEY, OR BREAK A CLUB TO FORCE ONE OPEN. SAVE A KEY FOR THE FRONT DOOR.

ALL ATTACKS CAN MISS. THE GUN IS MORE ACCURATE, AND ITS FIRST SHOT SURPRISES EACH CREATURE SO YOU CAN DODGE PAST SAFELY. EVERY SHOT COSTS A BULLET.

DEFEATED CREATURES SOMETIMES DROP FOOD, BULLETS, KEYS, OR STRANGER SUPPLIES. CLOTH CAN BIND A WOUND FOR 1 TO 2 HEALTH. TO THE VICTOR GO THE DUBIOUS SPOILS.

OLD MONSTERS REMEMBER OLD FEARS. SOMETIMES UNLIKELY POSSESSIONS BELONG TOGETHER.

USE < OR COMMA TO MOVE LEFT, AND > OR PERIOD TO MOVE RIGHT. THE ARROW KEYS ALSO MOVE LEFT AND RIGHT, OR TAKE VISIBLE STAIRS UP AND DOWN.

EVERY GAME ACTION COSTS ONE TURN. INVENTORY AND MENU KEYS ARE FREE.
